## The central question

> **How can distributed human knowledge, expertise, and participation become cumulative collective capacity without concentrating authority over truth, meaning, recognition, or coordination in a single institution, platform, or AI system?**

Konnaxion is the public coordination layer of a broader body of work, **kOA**, built around a simple problem:

Humanity does not lack intelligence. It repeatedly loses intelligence **between** contribution, knowledge, judgment, decision, execution, and memory.

The proposed pattern is:

```text
Contribution
  ↓
Provenance + attribution
  ↓
Structured meaning + explicit uncertainty
  ↓
Contextual expertise
  ↓
Deliberation
  ↓
Advice + legitimate decision
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Accountability
  ↓
Reusable memory
  ↓
Future capability
```

The architecture is designed so that the system itself remains contestable:

- no automatic truth authority;
- no universal expert caste;
- no AI with final civic authority;
- no mandatory global controller;
- portable knowledge artifacts;
- offline/local operation;
- self-hosting and credible exit;
- attribution preserved through aggregation;
- multilingual semantic governance;
- independent replication rather than permanent founder dependence.

## Why publish this publicly

A systems-change claim should not depend on a private pitch.

Publishing the dossier creates an inspectable object:

> **claim → source → evidence → challenge → revision**

This repository therefore separates:

- what is **implemented or publicly inspectable**;
- what is **documented architecture**;
- what is **partially functional**;
- what is a **scenario or research hypothesis**;
- what still requires **independent social proof**.

Core rule:

> **Scenario != Evidence.**

## Public request to Ashoka

This dossier includes a direct but bounded request to **Ashoka**.

Ashoka publicly describes its Fellowship criteria around a new idea that changes the pattern in a field, creativity, entrepreneurial quality, social impact, and ethical fiber. It also describes systems change as changing how a system operates, including roles, relationships, rules, resources, and results, with an emphasis on spreading impact beyond the entrepreneur's own organization.

Those criteria are unusually close to the questions this work is now ready to face.

## This is not a request for blanket endorsement

I am **not** asking Ashoka to endorse every kOA component, philosophical text, technical claim, or future scenario.

I am asking for serious engagement with a narrower proposition:

> **Does this work represent a credible systems-change trajectory, and what independent evidence would be required to establish that it can become a new social pattern?**

The strongest possible response is not praise.

It is a rigorous answer.

## Precedent: public, falsifiable engagement

This is not the first time I have made a proposition public before asking an institution to engage with it.

### Science Silk Road / Koali

A public research dossier proposes Koali as a source of falsifiable hypotheses about scientific capability formation, authority, provenance, dependency, and institutional reproduction. It explicitly treats field research—not the architecture—as the test.

https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/science-silk-road-koali

### Partners for Public Good / Koali pressure test

A second repository asks Partners for Public Good to pressure-test Koali against real government technology constraints. The invitation is deliberately adversarial in the constructive sense:

> **Don't endorse Koali. Try to break it.**

https://github.com/Rejean-McCormick/partners-for-public-good_pressure-test-koali

The same principle applies here:

> **Do not accept the systems-change claim because I make it. Test whether the evidence, trajectory, safeguards, and replication logic support it.**
