## Authority boundaries

- **Kristal Math** owns the global recognized mathematical corpus.
- **Kristal Grand Challenges** owns per-problem research state. Imports are rebuildable projections of a pinned global snapshot.
- **Formulator** orchestrates research and verification but does not silently write the global corpus.
- **AI relay output, finite tests and strategy statistics are not proof.**
- **Pi Theory is not part of this stack.** It is a separate speculative research program catalogued elsewhere.
