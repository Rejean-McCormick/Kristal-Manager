# Utility map

Les utilités ne sont pas des produits : elles regroupent des workflows transférables.

## Find & Understand
Faire passer un signal, une question ou une anomalie vers un contexte structuré, sourcé et interprétable.

- **Find the right expertise** — Connect a question, need, or project with the relevant people and knowledge.
- **Investigate a signal** — Move from a symptom, signal, or anomaly to structured understanding.
- **Reconstruct context and provenance** — Connect sources, history, versions, and relationships so people can see where a belief or claim comes from.

## Learn & Share
Transformer pratique, preuves et connaissances en apprentissage transmissible, adaptable et réutilisable.

- **Connect learning, practice, and evidence** — Carry learning through activity, evidence, and demonstrated situated capability.
- **Teach across language or connectivity constraints** — Preserve meaning and learning continuity when language, network access, or location changes.
- **Turn practice into transferable knowledge** — Capture a situated method and make it shareable, criticizable, and reusable.

## Collaborate & Create
Relier personnes, contexte, besoins, versions et responsabilités pendant la création collective.

- **Assemble a team around a problem** — Identify the required capabilities and form the right collective for a goal.
- **Co-create without losing the why** — Keep needs, references, trade-offs, and versions connected throughout collective creation.
- **Move an idea to a prototype** — Connect exploration, design, testing, decisions, and the next operational step.

## Choose & Govern
Comparer options, expertise, critères et perspectives avant une décision dont le mandat et les raisons restent visibles.

- **Compare and prioritize explicitly** — Place options, criteria, evidence, and constraints side by side before choosing.
- **Connect decision, mandate, and consequences** — Preserve why a choice was made through the work and outcomes it triggers.
- **Deliberate across perspectives** — Keep contributions, disagreements, expertise, and legitimacy visible before a collective decision.

## Organize & Act
Transformer objectifs, décisions et demandes en travail attribué, suivi et vérifiable.

- **Carry a plan through execution** — Turn an objective or project into tracked work and then into a verified result.
- **Coordinate roles and dependencies** — Maintain a shared work state across people, teams, resources, and constraints.
- **Deliver work with its context** — Give the responsible person the task, the why, the history, the constraints, and the expected evidence of completion.

## Respond & Coordinate
Maintenir une situation partagée et coordonner rapidement les responsabilités quand le contexte change ou se dégrade.

- **Keep working in field/offline mode** — Keep knowledge, roles, and work local, then synchronize without inventing success.
- **Maintain a changing shared situation** — Prevent teams from acting on different or outdated versions of reality.
- **Turn an incident into coordinated response** — Move quickly from signal to qualification, responsibilities, and action.

## Remember & Improve
Transformer actions, résultats, incidents et expériences en mémoire réutilisable et en meilleure capacité future.

- **Preserve capability beyond individuals** — Make knowledge, method, and evidence reproducible when people change roles or leave.
- **See patterns over time** — Compare cases, seasons, incidents, or measures to detect recurrence and change.
- **Turn action into a lesson** — Connect results, decisions, and causes into usable lessons learned.

## Disseminate & Connect to the Public
Publier, expliquer, enseigner et mettre à jour des contenus sans couper les sorties de leurs sources et versions.

- **Explain publicly without severing sources** — Make knowledge or decisions understandable while retaining access to context and provenance.
- **Keep media aligned with knowledge** — Connect published content, versions, and sources so it can be corrected, superseded, or revised.
- **Turn knowledge into media or learning** — Render a knowledge base as courses, video, audio, guides, libraries, or channels.
